================================================================================
                              MUSIC COMMANDS
================================================================================

!play <song>              - Play a song (costs points, free for VIP)
!dedicate @user <song>    - Dedicate a song to someone (costs points)
!next / !skip             - Skip current song (starts vote for regular users)
!instskip                 - Instant skip (costs 1000 points)
!skipvote-                - Cancel active skip vote (Admin only)
!stop                     - Stop playback and clear queue
!clear                    - Clear the queue (costs points based on queue size)
!current / !np            - Show currently playing song
!queue / !q               - Show songs in queue
!autoplay / !ap           - Toggle autoplay on/off (Admin only)
!maxduration <seconds>    - Set max song duration limit (Admin only)
!insert <song>            - Insert song to play next (Admin only)
!modes                    - Show available music modes
!mode <type>              - Switch music mode (e.g., !mode bollywood)
!stream                   - Get raw stream link in DM
!radiourl                 - Generate fresh radio URL (Admin only)
!lyrics                   - Get lyrics search link for current song
!music                    - Show music help/commands

================================================================================
                            ACCESS LEVELS
================================================================================

OWNER:
- Full access to all commands
- Can add/remove admins
- Can restart bot/server
- Access to BTM (Background Task Manager)

ADMIN:
- Free music commands (no point cost)
- Instant skip without voting
- Queue management (!clear, !insert)
- Audio settings (!volume, !quality, !announce)
- Mode management
- User management (!addvip, !removevip)
- System diagnostics

VIP:
- Free music commands (no point cost)
- Instant skip without voting
- !clear with 1-hour cooldown

REGULAR USER:
- Music commands cost points
- Skip starts a vote (40% needed)
- Limited access to settings

================================================================================
                           POINT COSTS
================================================================================

Use !costs to see current pricing. Points can be obtained by:
- Tipping gold to the bot
- Purchasing point packages (!packs)
- Receiving from admins (!givepoints)
